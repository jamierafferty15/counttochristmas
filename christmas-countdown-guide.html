Milestones on the way to 25 December

Christmas Countdown Guide

A Christmas countdown can be more useful than a single number. Different milestones make the run-up feel different, so this guide explains what the main countdown is showing and a few helpful points along the way.

12 weeks to go

Long enough for plans, travel and bigger purchases without the sense of urgency that arrives later.

8 weeks to go

A useful point to begin gift lists, booking festive events and thinking about anything that needs delivery.

4 weeks to go

The countdown starts to feel immediate. Advent is close, calendars appear and December plans begin to fill up.

24 days to go

The Advent Calendar becomes a daily ritual. On this site, one new door unlocks each day from 1 December.

2 weeks to go

A good moment to check deliveries, food plans and anything you have been confidently saying you would “do later”.

1 week to go

The final stretch: fewer plans, more finishing touches, and increasingly frequent use of the phrase “it’ll be fine”.

Weeks, days and sleeps

The homepage breaks the remaining time into complete weeks plus leftover days, then hours, minutes and seconds. The separate sleeps number rounds the total remaining time up to a whole night count.

Use the countdown however you like

For some people it is planning information. For others it is just a small moment of anticipation. The site is designed to do both without turning Christmas into a project-management dashboard.
