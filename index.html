About the countdown

Count to Christmas is a small place to enjoy the anticipation of 25 December. The live timer follows your device’s local clock and shows complete weeks, remaining days, hours, minutes and seconds until Christmas begins. The sleeps number rounds the time up to the next Christmas morning, which is why it feels a little more human than a stopwatch.

Make the scene your own with optional music and falling snow, open a new Advent Calendar door each day in December, enjoy the daily fact, or share the countdown with someone else looking forward to Christmas.
