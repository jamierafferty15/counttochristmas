About Count to Christmas

Count to Christmas is a simple festive countdown showing the time remaining until 25 December in weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds.

How the countdown works

The countdown uses the date and time on your device and calculates the time remaining until Christmas Day. After Christmas Day has passed, it automatically begins counting down to the following Christmas.

What else is included?

Alongside the main countdown, the site includes optional falling snow, festive music, a daily Christmas fact, sharing tools and a simple “sleeps until Christmas” message.

Why this site exists

The aim is to keep the experience lightweight, cheerful and easy to use on phones, tablets and desktop computers without requiring an account or sign-in.
