The simple answer behind the big number

How Many Sleeps Until Christmas?

“How many sleeps until Christmas?” is simply a friendlier way to ask how many nights remain before Christmas morning. It is especially popular with children because it turns an abstract number of days into something concrete: go to bed, wake up, and you are one sleep closer.

How this site calculates sleeps

Count to Christmas uses the date and time on your device. It calculates the time remaining until midnight at the start of 25 December and rounds that number up to the next whole day. So if there are 4 days and 3 hours left, the site shows 5 sleeps.

Why the sleeps number can differ from the days box

The main countdown separates complete weeks from the remaining days. For example, 23 total days might appear as 3 weeks and 2 days. The sleeps number shows the whole distance in nights instead, so the two displays answer slightly different questions.

What happens on Christmas Day?

Once Christmas Day begins, the sleeps number reaches zero and the main timer pauses at zero with a Christmas greeting. After 26 December begins, the site automatically starts counting down to the following Christmas.

A more human countdown

Seconds are precise, but sleeps are memorable. That is why the homepage gives the sleeps number its own prominent place alongside the detailed timer.
